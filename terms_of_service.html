1. 服务说明

本应用为用药管理工具，提供以下功能：

  • 用药提醒：设置定时提醒，支持每日、间隔、多时段等多种频率模式。
  • 用药记录：记录每次服药情况，包括完成、漏服、推迟等状态。
  • 药品扫描：使用相机扫描药品包装，智能识别药品名称，支持简体中文、繁体中文、英文。
  • 药品管理：添加、编辑、删除药品信息，支持自定义剂量、单位、备注。
  • 完成率统计：查看今日、本周、本月的服药完成率，并以直观的环形进度图展示。
  • 主屏幕小组件：在主屏幕快速查看今日用药提醒，一键完成用药记录。
  • 数据导出：导出用药记录数据，格式为 JSON，便于备份和数据分析。该功能属于订阅功能。

1. Service Description

This application is a medication management tool that provides the following features:

  • Medication Reminders: Set scheduled reminders with multiple frequency modes, including daily, interval, and multi-time slots.
  • Medication Tracking: Record each medication dose, including completed, missed, and postponed statuses.
  • Medication Scanning: Use your camera to scan medication packaging and intelligently recognize medication names (supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English).
  • Medication Management: Add, edit, and delete medication information with support for custom dosage, units, and notes.
  • Completion Rate Statistics: View today's, this week's, and this month's medication completion rates in an intuitive circular progress chart.
  • Home Screen Widget: Quickly view today's medication reminders on your Home Screen and complete medication records with one tap.
  • Data Export: Export medication record data in JSON format for backup and data analysis (Premium feature).
